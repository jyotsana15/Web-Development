Gradient Background Showcase

Linear Gradient Background

This section uses a linear gradient, transitioning colors smoothly from one direction to another. This particular gradient goes from top to bottom, blending shades of blue and green.

Radial Gradient Background

This section features a radial gradient, where colors emanate from a central point outwards. Here, the gradient starts with a light yellow in the center and fades to an orange at the edges, creating a sun-like effect.

Parallax Effect with Fixed Background

This section also uses a linear gradient, but with a fixed background attachment. As you scroll, the background image (or in this case, the gradient) stays in place, creating a subtle parallax scrolling effect. This gives a sense of depth to the page content.
